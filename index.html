About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics and Data Science at Washington University in St. Louis, where I am honored to be advised by Prof. Chao Cheng.

My research interests lie in causal inference and semiparametric theory, as well as statistical machine learning and Bayesian computation.

Before joining WashU, I received an M.A. in Statistics from Columbia University and a B.S. in Financial Mathematics and Statistics from the University of California, Santa Barbara.
